# Retail-Sales-Profit-Analysis
Power BI dashboard for retail sales, profit, discount and regional analysis

Tools Used:
Power BI
Power Query
DAX

Dashboard Features:
Total Sales and Total Profit
Profit Margin Analysis
Sales by Region
Sales by Product
Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis
Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products

Project Objective:
To analyze retail sales data and identify sales trends, profitable products, and regional performance.

Key Insights:
Smartphone has the highest sales among the displayed top 5 products.
The dashboard compares sales and profit across different months.
Regional sales are compared using a doughnut chart.
